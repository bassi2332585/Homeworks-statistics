Trajectories of Total Score $S_n$ ($m$ Attackers)

1. Total Score: $S_n$

$E[S_n] = np$
$Var(S_n) = np(1-p)$
Variance grows linearly with $n$.

2. Average Score: $S_n / n$ (LLN)

$E[S_n/n] = p$
$Var(S_n/n) = \frac{p(1-p)}{n} \to 0$
Collapses to $p$ (LLN).

3. Raw Norm: $S_n / \sqrt{n}$

$E[S_n/\sqrt{n}] = p\sqrt{n} \to +\infty$
$Var(S_n/\sqrt{n}) = p(1-p)$
Constant Var, but drifts to $+\infty$!

4. Standardized $Z_n$ (CLT)

$Z_n = \frac{S_n - np}{\sqrt{np(1-p)}}$
$E[Z_n] = 0, Var(Z_n) = 1$
Converges to $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ (CLT).

Symmetric Random Walk Trajectories ($S_n$)

1. Total Score: $S_n$

$E[S_n] = 0$
$Var(S_n) = n$
Symmetric, variance grows as $n$.

2. Average Score: $S_n / n$ (LLN)

$E[S_n/n] = 0$
$Var(S_n/n) = \frac{1}{n} \to 0$
Collapses to $0$ (LLN).

3. Raw Norm: $S_n / \sqrt{n}$ (CLT)

$E[S_n/\sqrt{n}] = 0$
$Var(S_n/\sqrt{n}) = 1$
Naturally converges to $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$!

4. Standardized $Z_n$ (CLT)

$Z_n = \frac{S_n - 0}{\sqrt{n \cdot 1}} = \frac{S_n}{\sqrt{n}}$
$E[Z_n] = 0, Var(Z_n) = 1$
Identical to $S_n/\sqrt{n}$ since $\mu=0$.

4. Theoretical Synthesis & Model Limitations

Formal mathematical properties of normalizations and conceptual role of $n$ vs $m$.

💡 Conceptual Clarification: Role of $n$ vs. Role of $m$
Number of Systems / Steps ($n$): Governs the asymptotic limit physics of a single trajectory. Increasing $n$ drives convergence in LLN ($S_n/n \to \mu$) and CLT ($Z_n \to \mathcal{N}(0,1)$).
Number of Attackers / Trajectories ($m$): Represents the Monte Carlo sample size. Increasing $m$ does not change the theoretical probability $p$ or single-attacker physics (no inter-attacker interaction/collusion modeled). It simply increases histogram resolution and empirical stability (Glivenko-Cantelli Theorem).
Quantity Formula Expected Value $E[\cdot]$ Variance $Var(\cdot)$ Behavior as $n \to \infty$
Total Score $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ $n\mu$ $n\sigma^2$ Variance grows linearly ($n\sigma^2 \to \infty$). Distribution spreads.
Average Score (LLN) $S_n / n$ $\mu$ $\frac{\sigma^2}{n} \to 0$ Converges in probability to constant mean $\mu$ (LLN).
Raw Normalized Score $S_n / \sqrt{n}$ $\mu\sqrt{n}$ $\sigma^2$ (Constant) Variance is constant, but if $\mu \ne 0$, distribution drifts to $+\infty$.
Centered Standardized (CLT) $Z_n = \frac{S_n - n\mu}{\sigma\sqrt{n}}$ $0$ $1$ Converges in distribution to Standard Normal $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ (CLT).

Limitations of the Penetration Model in Real-World Cybersecurity

  • Independent & Identically Distributed (i.i.d.) Assumption: Real system defenses adapt dynamically. Penetrating layer $i$ often yields credentials that make layer $i+1$ substantially easier to breach (cascading failure).
  • Lack of Attacker Interaction: The model treats all $m$ attackers as completely independent trajectories. In reality, attackers share exploits (0-day intelligence) or compete for limited server resources.
  • Stationary Probabilities: Defense mechanisms (SIEM, IDS/IPS, automated IP blocking) increase defense strength after initial detection, making $p$ non-stationary over time $i$.
